# Everything it can do

Gathered from every chapter, in one place.

## Overall

+ Run on your own Windows computer, in a window of its own, with no account, no administrator rights and no internet needed; update itself when online, from signed releases, when you press the button; install and uninstall with one file each way; keep your data in the folder you choose, and move it later from Settings.
+ Keep every run, export register, decision and setting between sessions, with nothing to save by hand; reopen the run you were last working on.
+ Keep every file you give it, named for what it is and filed by kind, and let you open, save, share, zip or find any of them in File Explorer.
+ Explain every open entry in plain words: what it is, what was checked, the closest match and why it was not taken, what it means in rupees, and what to do.
+ Let you tick entries off (done, being chased, not a problem), undo that, and link straight to an entry.
+ Read Excel, CSV, text PDFs, scanned PDFs, photos and screenshots - scans offline, in about a second a page.

## Reconciliation - GST

+ Match your Tally purchase register with one or many GSTR-2B files in six passes: exact, amount differs, bill number differs, probably the same bill, entered in parts, and one-sided.
+ Handle amendments, credit and debit notes, ISD and imports; leading zeros and separators in bill numbers; M/s and company endings in names.
+ Check every bill for place of supply, reverse charge, portal-blocked credit, partial credit, impossible or mixed tax rates, IGST mixed with CGST/SGST, values that do not tie, duplicates, and Section 16(4) lapse.
+ Work out the credit you can safely claim (each bill's credit taken off once, at the first deduction that applies), the credit waiting on bookings, purchases your suppliers have not filed, and the last date to claim; and, with your sales files, a GSTR-3B working set off against the credit allowed by law.
+ Match your sales register with the GSTR-1 you filed.
+ Produce a 17-to-27-sheet Excel workbook with a health score and supplier grades, a signed-off PDF working paper, CSV extracts, and a letter to every supplier with open bills; re-match with different settings without re-uploading.

## Reconciliation - Bank

+ Read statements from any bank in Excel, CSV, PDF, scan or photo; recognise the bank, account and IFSC; classify every line's channel; check the running balance row by row.
+ Match by reference, by amount and date, as timing differences, as amounts that differ, and one bank line against several book entries.
+ Produce a bank reconciliation statement that proves itself to the rupee, name unpresented cheques, uncredited deposits, charges, interest and direct debits, and flag stale cheques, duplicate payments, large cash and unexplained credits.

## Reconciliation - a party's statement

+ Read suppliers' and customers' statements in any format, including Tally's own quirks, one Amount column with a Dr/Cr column, two-sided layouts and interest statements; pick the right ledger from a workbook of many.
+ Match by document number, amount and date, and interest and TDS as period totals; build a bridge that names every rupee between the two balances.
+ Check an interest statement's arithmetic line by line; explain an interest difference; check a supplier's bills against what they filed in GSTR-2B.
+ Draft a balance confirmation letter, ready to copy or email, and remember the supplier's contact.

## Exports

+ Keep one register per exporter from any mix of shipping bills, bills of lading, invoices, GSTR-1 (JSON or Excel), bank advices, DGFT IRM and eBRC lists, ICEGATE status, refund and drawback downloads, the bank's EDPMS statement and your own shipping bill register.
+ Show, per invoice, the shipping bill, bill of lading, Table 6A line, payment, eBRC, IGST refund and drawback, with everything on file in the invoice's file and its papers in one zip.
+ Work out every payment deadline under the right one of four FEMA regimes, from the date your bank uses, honouring the bank's extensions.
+ Count payments only on evidence, suggest the rest and wait for your click; ask which invoices a newly added bank advice pays, and split one payment over as many invoices as you choose; spot a payment that covers several of one buyer's invoices at once, and an advance booked for goods already shipped; prepare the DGFT bulk eBRC sheet with every row checked.
+ Take your own yes or no on whether an invoice is in GSTR-1 and whether its IGST refund and drawback came back, where no document says or one is wrong.
+ Show what was read from every document you add - its numbers, dates, money and where it will be filed - and keep it out of the register until you post it.
+ Correct anything read from any document, before or after it is posted, field by field and row by row, and turn a misread or unrecognised file into the kind of document it is.
+ Read a document again with a newer version's reader, keeping your corrections that still differ.
+ With the AI checker (an add-on), have a local AI model read every document again in the background and point out where its reading differs from the app's - never changing anything until you say so.
+ Offer the ICEGATE, DGFT and bank lists you save to your Downloads folder, and give each shipping bill to look up a link to ICEGATE's enquiry with its port, number and date ready to copy.
+ Keep a history of everything done to a register - each document added, corrected (before and after), posted and removed, each payment placed, each answer and decision - with when and who, on the page and in the Excel download.
+ Catch Table 6A mismatches (SB001, SB003, SB005, IGST, WPAY/LUT, missing, duplicated), missing papers, refunds and drawback not received with their ICEGATE codes, drawback waiting on proof of payment, and EDPMS disagreements.
+ Draft the SB005 pack per port, the Table 9A worksheet, the SB003 undertaking, the EDPMS declaration, the extension request and the ICEGATE look-up list; give back the register in your own sheet's layout.
