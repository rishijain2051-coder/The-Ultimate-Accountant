Invoice Bridge

Upload a bill, say what kind of entry it is, check what the app read side by side with the bill itself, and post it to TallyPrime with one click. It reads printed and handwritten bills, in English and in Hindi, and everything runs on this computer.

Guide for version 1.0.0

What it does, and what it never does

  1. You add a bill: a PDF, a scan or a photo from your phone, one or a batch.
  2. You say what it is: Purchase, Sales, Payment, Receipt, Contra, Journal, Debit or Credit Note, or any voucher type of your own in Tally.
  3. You review it side by side: the bill on the left; on the right, what the app read, the exact entry it will make in Tally, a search for duplicates in Tally, and the arithmetic and GST checks.
  4. You post it to Tally, save it there as a draft, or export it as a file - only when you say so.
Nothing leaves this computer. The app, its database, the text reader (OCR) and the AI reader all run here. It uses the internet only to download the AI reader while it is installed, and to check for updates.
The AI only reads. It copies what is written on the bill, exactly as written, including handwriting and Hindi, and reads each scan a second time to check the text reader. It does not chat, translate, calculate, guess, choose ledgers or tax, judge duplicates, or post anything. Every value it read is shown in amber for you to confirm.
Nothing is posted to Tally without you. Posting is off until you switch it on in Settings, and even then each entry is posted only when you press the button.

Installing

  1. Run Install.exe. If you received it zipped instead, unzip it first. Windows may warn that the program is not recognised, because it is not code-signed: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Choose where to install it. Any folder you can write to works, for example D:\Apps on another drive. It needs about 8 GB free. A folder that needs administrator rights, such as C:\Program Files, is refused with a message rather than failing half way through.
  3. Keep the desktop shortcut if you want one.
  4. It downloads the AI reader (about 5 GB, the first time only) in a window that shows the progress, then it can start the app. If the internet drops, the app still works without the AI: run Install.exe again later and the download continues where it stopped.

It installs everything it needs - its own Python, the text reader, the AI runtime and the AI model - so the computer needs nothing else. It is listed in Add/Remove Programs. Running a newer Install.exe updates it in place, and closes the app first if it is open.

Your data - the database, the bills you gave it, exported files, backups and logs - is kept in %APPDATA%\Ultimate Accountant\bridge (or, after an older stand-alone install, %APPDATA%\TallyInvoiceBridge\data), wherever the program itself is installed. The Start menu has Open my data folder. Uninstalling never removes it.

Starting and closing

Start it from its shortcut. It opens in a window of its own. Closing the window stops everything the app started. Opening it again while it is running brings the open window to the front.

Connecting Tally

  1. Open TallyPrime with your company loaded.
  2. Press F1 (Help) → Settings → Connectivity → Client/Server configuration. Set TallyPrime acts as to Both and the port to 9000. (Tally.ERP 9: F12 → Advanced Configuration.)
  3. In the app, open Settings, press Discover companies, then Sync each company.
  4. To post from the app, tick Allow this app to write to Tally in Settings. Until you do, the app only reads from Tally and exports files.

Sync copies your ledgers, stock items and voucher types to this computer, so reading and reviewing work even with Tally closed. Tally needs to be open only to sync, to search it for duplicates, and to post. Sync again after you create ledgers in Tally.

Tally on another computer on the same Wi-Fi or network: put that computer's address in Host in Settings (its IPv4 address from ipconfig, such as 192.168.1.6). Windows Firewall on the Tally computer must let port 9000 in. Once, in PowerShell run as administrator on the Tally computer, with this computer's own address at the end of the first line:

New-NetFirewallRule -DisplayName "TallyPrime 9000" -Direction Inbound `
    -Protocol TCP -LocalPort 9000 -RemoteAddress 192.168.1.15
Get-NetFirewallRule -Direction Inbound -Action Block |
    Where-Object { $_.DisplayName -match 'tally' } | Remove-NetFirewallRule

The second command removes the rule Windows makes if its firewall question about TallyPrime was ever cancelled. Then opening http://192.168.1.6:9000 in a browser on this computer says TallyPrime Server is Running. Tally's port has no password, which is why the rule lets in only this computer.

Several companies. Every company open in Tally is found, and each keeps its own ledgers. A bill goes to the company whose GSTIN is on it, or else to the one you chose at upload. One that names none of them, such as a kachcha bill, is never guessed: it waits for you to choose the company on the review screen.

Reading a bill

The first screen: drop bills here or choose them. A folder is also watched: anything saved into it is read on its own.

  1. Add document. Drop the files or press Choose files.
  2. What is it? Pick the voucher type. The list is your own Tally's; a type you made yourself is marked with the built-in type it works like (a "GST Purchase - Services" posts like a Purchase). With several companies, leave Company on From your GSTIN on each bill, or pick one - do pick one for kachcha bills. For a scan of several bills in one PDF, tick Each page is a separate bill.
  3. Review. The app reads the bill and opens it for review. A printed PDF takes a second or two; a scan or a photo is read by the AI and takes longer - see "It uses at most half of the computer". In a batch, press Review the ready ones to start on them while the rest are read.

The review screen

A Hindi kachcha bill with handwritten figures, read by the AI. The fields in amber are the ones it read; the supplier's ledger is still to be picked. The line items table follows below the panels.

On the left: the bill

Exactly as you gave it, page by page. Fit, 125% and 175% zoom; Original opens the file itself.

① What I read

Every field the app found: bill number, date, the supplier or customer, their GSTIN, place of supply, taxable value, CGST, SGST, IGST, round-off and the total. Hover over a label to see the line of the bill it came from. Amber means the AI read it: check it against the bill. Correct anything, then Save changes.

The ledger in Tally is chosen from your synced ledgers. The app suggests one: by GSTIN first (a certain match), then by the choice you made for the same name before, then by how similar the name is, with its confidence and the reason. When it cannot tell, it asks. Create this ledger in Tally makes a new one from the bill's name and GSTIN.

② What I'll post to Tally

The voucher exactly as Tally will receive it, as debit and credit rows, built by the same code that posts it. It must balance. Anything still missing - a ledger to pick - is shown in red and named under the table.

A printed Hindi tax invoice written in Devanagari digits. The AI misread the bill number (११२ as 192), a rate (४४ as 48) and the total (३४४४ as 3484), and missed the tax rows. The checks caught the rate, the total and the missing tax; nothing can catch a wrong bill number by arithmetic, which is why every number on such a bill needs a look. It cannot be posted until the figures are corrected or the checks knowingly accepted.

③ Duplicate check

The bill is compared with every entry in this app and with the vouchers already in Tally around its date, including ones typed in by hand.

  • Likely duplicate - the same bill number is already there. It is not posted until you tick "this is a different bill".
  • Similar bill - same party and amount, different number. A warning only.

Tally is searched again at the moment of posting. If it cannot be searched then, the app says so instead of posting blind. Every override is recorded.

④ Checks

Arithmetic and GST, never opinion: the lines add up to the taxable value (or, on a bill with no tax, to the total); quantity times rate is the amount; IGST or CGST+SGST is right for the supplier's state and the place of supply; the GSTIN's check digit is valid; taxable value plus tax is the total. A failed check is shown in red and must be fixed or knowingly accepted.

Line items

Where each line posts: an expense or purchase ledger, or a stock item. Your choice is remembered, so the same item on the next bill is matched by itself.

The buttons

Post to TallyCreates the voucher in Tally now.
Save as draft in TallyCreates it as an optional voucher: it shows in the Day Book but does not touch your books until you make it regular in Tally.
Export XMLWrites a file you can import into Tally yourself.
Show XMLShows exactly what would be sent, without sending it.
RejectSets the bill aside.

Each asks you to confirm. After posting, change the voucher in Tally; the app does not sync edits back.

Handwritten and Hindi bills

A scan or a photo is read by the AI from the picture itself, which is what makes handwriting and Hindi readable at all. Printed text is also read by the text reader; where the two readings of a value differ, the app shows both and marks the value uncertain.

  • Names stay as written. A supplier written as श्री गणेश ट्रेडर्स stays that way - the AI does not translate. The first bill from such a supplier needs you to pick their ledger once; after that the app matches the name by itself.
  • Numbers are copied, then checked. On a bill without tax the lines must add up to the total, which catches most misread figures.
  • Devanagari digits need a careful look. On bills written in Devanagari digits the AI confuses ४, ८, ६, ९ and ५ (4, 8, 6, 9, 5) more often than it should. Check every number on such bills against the paper.
  • A photo works. Take it straight on, in good light, with the whole bill in the picture.

An entry with anything the AI read is never counted as "clean", so it is never approved in a batch without you looking at it.

The review list

Review lists every entry: to review, exported, drafts in Tally, posted and rejected, weakest first, with a search by bill number, party or GSTIN. Export N clean exports in one go the entries that scored high, failed no check, have no likely duplicate and have nothing read by the AI.

While a batch is being read, the list says how many are still to come, and each bill joins it when it is ready; the bill you have open is left alone. A bill that could not be read is shown at the top with the reason, Try again and Set aside. Closing the app in the middle of a batch is safe: it carries on at the next start. With several companies, each entry shows its company, or Choose company.

It uses at most half of the computer

The app, the text reader and the AI together use no more than half of the computer, so Tally and everything else stay quick. Measured every second while reading bills on a laptop with an i7-13650HX and an RTX 4050:

LimitMeasured
Processor (CPU)50%, enforced by Windowshighest second 46.5%, average 33%
Graphics card memory50%45.5% at most
Graphics card busy time50% on average19% on average

The graphics card still reaches full use for fractions of a second while it reads; no graphics card offers a way to prevent that. The price of the limits is time: a one-page scan takes about 40 seconds with a graphics card and about a minute without one. Printed PDFs take a second or two either way.

Settings

Settings: Tally, the AI reader, review, the watched folder and backups.

  • Tally: its address and port, Discover companies, Sync per company, and Allow this app to write to Tally.
  • AI reader: on or off. With it off, only printed text is read, by rules.
  • Review: the score at which an entry counts as clean.
  • Watched folder: bills saved into it are read on their own.
  • Backups: the database is backed up every six hours and the last 14 are kept; Back up now makes one on the spot.

When something is not right

Tally isn't connectedOpen TallyPrime with the company loaded and check step 2 of "Connecting Tally". A dialog open inside Tally also stops it answering. Tally on another computer: its firewall is the usual cause - see "Connecting Tally".
A bill could not be readThe review list says why. Try again reads it again; a file deleted from the data folder has to be uploaded again.
The AI is not availableHover over AI at the top right to see why. The app keeps working on printed bills; scans come in with empty fields for you to fill in.
A ledger is missingCreate it in Tally (or with Create this ledger in Tally), then Sync in Settings and Re-match on the entry.
The app does not startA message says so. The details are in the logs folder inside the data folder (Start menu → Open my data folder).

What it cannot do yet

  • Reading from your TallyPrime is tested; posting into it is not yet. Make the first entries with Show XML and Save as draft before posting for real.
  • Its reading of real handwritten Hindi bills has not been measured yet: until it has, check every value on such bills.
  • TallyPrime has no way to attach the bill itself to a voucher; the bill stays in the app, found by its number.
  • Edits made in Tally after posting do not come back to the app.
