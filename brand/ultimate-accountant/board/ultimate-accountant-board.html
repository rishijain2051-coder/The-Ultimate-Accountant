What we heard

The brief

The house the two apps live in: one program for a small Indian business's books - its bills and bank statements posted into Tally, and the month checked against GSTR-2B, the bank and its parties. For the owner as much as the accountant: plain words, nothing uploaded. A sibling of Assurance Console's maroon and Invoice Bridge's ink, in the gold both share.

finalexactplainIndian

Success: says finality without a tick, a shield or a rupee sign; clear at 16 px; one colour; at home beside the two marks it holds.

Concept A

Nothing More

/- : what is written after an amount so that nothing can be added to it. The last word on a figure.

  • Every Indian bill and cheque ends its amount with it: the figure is final, as the books should be
  • A 60 degree slash and a bar, flat ends - the family's vocabulary - and it half-reads as an A
  • The gold Assurance Console and Invoice Bridge share, as the ground both live on, with their ink
